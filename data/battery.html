Hardware ADC Pins, Wiring & Voltage Divider Guide

1. ESP32-WROOM-32 ADC Pin Selection (ADC1 vs ADC2 WiFi Restriction)

  • Crucial WiFi Limitation: You MUST use an ADC1 pin. ADC2 pins (GPIO 0, 2, 4, 12, 13, 14, 15, 25, 26, 27) are shared with the internal WiFi radio and cannot be read while WiFi is running.
  • Recommended Best Pins (ADC1): GPIO 34 or GPIO 35 (Ideal input-only pins). Other valid ADC1 pins: GPIO 32, GPIO 33, GPIO 36 (VP), GPIO 39 (VN).

1. ESP32-S3 Analog Battery Sense Pins

  • WiFi Compatibility: Unlike original ESP32, both ADC1 and ADC2 work concurrently with WiFi on ESP32-S3.
  • Recommended Analog Pins: GPIO 1, GPIO 2, GPIO 3, GPIO 4, GPIO 5, GPIO 6, GPIO 7, GPIO 8, GPIO 9, GPIO 10.

Voltage Safety Warning: ESP32 pins are destroyed by voltages above 3.3V! Always measure voltage through the resistor divider circuit.

3. Voltage Divider Circuitry

The divider scales battery voltage down to a safe range (under 3.0V max):

(BATTERY +) ---- [ R1 ] ---- (ESP32 ADC PIN) ---- [ R2 ] ---- (GND / BATTERY -)
  • Standard R2 = 10kΩ. R1 is auto-calculated based on cell count (e.g., 33kΩ for 3S LiPo).
  • High total resistance (R1 + R2 > 20kΩ) ensures standby power draw is under 0.5 mA.
WARNING: Incorrect wiring or pin selection can permanently damage your ESP32.
  • Use an **Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC)** pin that can tolerate up to 3.3V.
  • Consult your specific ESP32 board's documentation to find a suitable ADC pin.
  • Double-check your wiring before applying power.
